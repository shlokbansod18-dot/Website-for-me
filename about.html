The Atelier — since 2019

A run closes when the cloth runs out

Velora was started in a two-room studio in Mumbai with a single bolt of cupro-silk and a stubborn idea: that a garment should be designed around how the cloth wants to fall, not around a delivery calendar.

Two hundred per run✦ No restock✦ No markdown✦ No repeats✦

Process — 001

From bolt to box

Roughly eleven weeks

01

Cloth first

We buy the cloth before there is a pattern. Five mills, the same five every season, and whatever they can spin that year sets the size of the run.

02

Drape on the stand

Every piece is draped on a half-scale stand before it is drawn. If the cloth will not hold the line on the stand, the line changes — not the cloth.

03

Three toiles

Three fittings on three different bodies. A piece that only works on one of them does not go into the run.

04

Cut in one place

The whole run is cut and sewn in a single atelier so the hand stays consistent from the first unit to the two-hundredth.

05

Retire the pattern

When the cloth is gone the pattern is archived and never re-cut. It is the only way we know to keep a limited run honest.

Position — 003

What we will not do

No virgin synthetics

Not in the shell, not in the lining, not in the thread. Recycled cupro is as far as we go, and we say so on the label.

No markdown cycle

A run is priced once. If it sells out in a week it sold out; if it takes a year, it takes a year. Nothing goes to a discount rail.

No mid-season filler

Fourteen pieces, twice a year. Anything that exists only to fill a gap in the calendar does not get made.
